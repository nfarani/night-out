Owlabout turns a neighbourhood and a mood into a timed plan: where to start, where to go next and where to end up, each stop a short walk from the last. Made for the parts of town you don’t know yet, and the nights nobody wants to organise

Good to know

Venues come from OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can edit, with chains filtered out. Well-known places, ones with their own Wikipedia entry or a listed building, come up a little more often. Places marked “Recommended by local guides” are ones several independent guides and reviews agree on. No venue pays to be here

About a quarter of venues list opening hours. When one doesn’t, the plan says so, so check before you go
